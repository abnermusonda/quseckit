# Contributing to QuSecKit

QuSecKit is in the design stage. The most valuable contributions right now are:

- **Design feedback** on the [whitepaper](docs/whitepaper.md): open an issue describing what is missing, wrong or over-scoped.
- **Remediation patterns:** real-world PQC fixes you have made (TLS configuration, library upgrades, certificate reissue, hybrid ML-KEM rollouts) that could become reusable templates.
- **Design partners:** teams willing to load a real CBOM and business map into v0.1.

## Ground rules

- Never include private keys, secrets or customer data in issues, pull requests or test fixtures.
- Report security issues privately to the maintainers rather than in a public issue.
- By contributing, you agree that your contributions are licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
